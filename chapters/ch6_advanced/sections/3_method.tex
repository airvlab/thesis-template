\section{Long Tables}\label{ch6sec:longtables}

Standard \texttt{table} environments cannot break across pages. For tables that span multiple pages, use the \texttt{longtable} package. Long tables repeat the header row at the top of each page and can include captions that span the full width.

\subsection{Using longtable}

The \texttt{longtable} environment replaces the \texttt{table} and \texttt{tabular} pair. Place the column definitions, header rows, and body rows inside a single \texttt{longtable} environment. \LaTeX{} will automatically split it across pages and repeat the header as needed.

\subsection{Notes in Long Tables}

You can combine \texttt{longtable} with \texttt{threeparttable} to add notes at the bottom of the table. Be careful with page breaks: notes should usually appear only on the final page of the table, or be repeated according to your style guide.
