\section{Summary}\label{ch6sec:advanced-summary}

This chapter introduced advanced topics for authors who need more than the standard chapter structure. Appendices are added in the postamble, long tables use the \texttt{longtable} package, and custom floats can be declared with \texttt{newfloat}. These features extend the template without changing its core conventions. The next chapter concludes the document and points to further help.
