\section{Introduction}\label{ch4sec:custom-intro}

Every thesis has different visual requirements. Some institutions mandate specific margins, others require a particular logo on the title page, and many authors want to adjust the colour scheme. This chapter explains where each design decision is controlled and how to change it safely.
