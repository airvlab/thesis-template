\subsection{Headers and Footers}

Running headers are controlled by the \texttt{fancyhdr} package in the class file. The default style prints the chapter title on the left and the page number on the right of odd pages, and vice versa on even pages. You can change the header content by redefining \verb|\chaptermark| or by editing the \texttt{fancyhdr} setup directly.
