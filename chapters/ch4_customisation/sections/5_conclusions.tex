\section{Summary}\label{ch4sec:custom-summary}

This chapter explained how to customise the visual design of the template. Colours, fonts, and page geometry are controlled in the class file; logos are set in the title-page preamble; and headers/footers are configured through \texttt{fancyhdr}. By centralising these decisions, the template keeps your chapter source focused on content rather than formatting. The next chapter covers building and publishing the thesis.
